Test 2: Check Pragati Hospital
────────────────────────────────────
PGPASSWORD="Abhishek@24" psql -U postgres -h localhost -d finclarity_prod \
  -c "SELECT name, (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM hospital.hospital_attributes 
      WHERE hospital_id = hospitals.id) as attrs 
      FROM hospital.hospitals WHERE name ILIKE '%pragati%';"

Expected: Pragati Hospital | 30


Test 3: Check Ganga Hospital
────────────────────────────────────
PGPASSWORD="Abhishek@24" psql -U postgres -h localhost -d finclarity_prod \
  -c "SELECT name, 
      (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM hospital.hospital_attributes 
       WHERE hospital_id = hospitals.id) as attrs,
      (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM hospital.hospital_panels 
       WHERE hospital_id = hospitals.id) as panels
      FROM hospital.hospitals WHERE name ILIKE '%ganga%';"

Expected: Ganga Hospital | 24 | 2
